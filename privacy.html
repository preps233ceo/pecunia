Pecunia

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

In short

Pecunia is a personal finance app for Android and the web, made by Preps233. Pecunia has no servers of its own. Your financial data is stored only on your phone, or in a spreadsheet in your own Google Drive if you choose to sign in with Google. We never see, sell or share your data, and the app contains no ads or third-party analytics.

What the app stores

The information you enter or that the app records for you: transactions (amount, date, account, category, description, payee), accounts and balances, budgets, projects, planned purchases, your display name and currency.

You choose where it is kept:

  • On this phone: in the app's private storage. Nothing leaves the device.
  • My Google account: in a spreadsheet called “Pecunia – Data” that the app creates in your own Google Drive, so you can also use Pecunia on the web.

SMS messages

With your permission, the Android app reads incoming and past SMS on your phone to find mobile money, bank and card transaction alerts. From those messages it records the amount, direction, account, date, payee, fees and balance, plus the alert text for your own reference.

  • Messages that are not financial alerts are ignored and are never stored or transmitted.
  • Recorded alerts are saved only where you chose to keep your data (your phone or your own Google Drive).
  • SMS data is never sent to us or to anyone else, and is never used for advertising.
  • You can turn SMS capture off at any time in the app (More → SMS capture) or in Android settings. The rest of the app works without it.

Google sign-in and Google Drive

If you choose your Google account, Pecunia asks for your email address (to show which account is signed in) and the drive.file permission. This lets Pecunia create and use only the files it creates. It cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.

Pecunia's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide the app's features to you and is not transferred to anyone else, used for advertising, or read by humans.

Notifications

The app can show a notification when it records a transaction while closed. You can turn this off in the app or in Android settings.

Deleting your data

  • Phone storage: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings.
  • Google account: delete the “Pecunia – Data” spreadsheet from your Google Drive. You can remove Pecunia's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Pecunia does not create accounts on any server, so there is nothing else to delete.

Security

Data sent between the app and Google uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Data on your phone is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

Children

Pecunia is meant for adults managing their own money and is not directed at children under 13.
