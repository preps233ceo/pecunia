2.What Pecunia is

Pecunia is a personal finance tool. It helps you record income, spending and transfers, read transaction alerts from your mobile money, bank and card providers, set budgets and plan savings. It is for your own personal, non-commercial use.

Pecunia is not a bank, a payment service or a financial adviser. It cannot move money, make payments or access your accounts. Its figures, charts, tips and savings plans are estimates based on what you record, provided for your information only. They are not financial, tax or investment advice. Check important decisions with a qualified professional.

3.Your data and accuracy

Your records belong to you. They are stored on your phone, or in a spreadsheet in your own Google Drive if you choose Google storage. We do not hold a copy.

Pecunia reads amounts, payees and accounts from alerts automatically, and alert formats differ between providers and change over time. Automatic records can be incomplete or wrong, for example a missed alert, a misread amount or a wrong category. Please review what Pecunia records and correct it where needed; your provider's own statement is the authoritative record of your account.

You are responsible for keeping your phone and Google account secure, and for keeping a backup of data stored only on your phone. If you uninstall the app or clear its data without a backup, data stored only on the phone cannot be recovered by us.
