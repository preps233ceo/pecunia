What is Pecunia?

Most of us get paid and pay through several channels at once: mobile money, one or two bank accounts, a card, and cash. Keeping track by hand is tiring, so by the end of the month it's hard to say where the money went. Pecunia brings it all into one place.

On Android, Pecunia reads the transaction alerts your MoMo, bank and card providers send you, by SMS or as app notifications, and turns them into records automatically. You add cash in seconds. Pecunia then gives you budgets, savings plans and clear charts of your spending.

What Pecunia does

Everything you need to see, plan and control your money.

Your SMS does the typing

MoMo, bank and card alerts become transactions automatically, with amount, account, payee and date filled in.

Bank app alerts too

Banks that alert you in their app instead of by SMS are covered. If a payment arrives both ways, it's recorded once.

Sorted for you

Type “groceries” or “fuel” in your MoMo reference and the payment lands in that category. Rules like “Shoprite → Food” do the rest.

Cash in seconds

Log cash spending with one tap. An optional evening reminder makes sure nothing slips through.

Budgets that carry forward

Set a monthly limit per category and see how close you are. Changes apply from that month on.

Plan projects & purchases

See how much to save each month for a project or something you want to buy, and whether your savings can cover it.

Interactive dashboard

On the web, explore all your history: click any chart to filter by month, category, payee, account or weekday.

Morning summary

A short note each morning: what you spent yesterday, the biggest item, and how the month is going.

Made for Africa

Ready-made MoMo and bank presets and currencies for Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, Uganda, South Africa and more. Themes from OLED black to light.

How it works

  1. Set up in a minute

    Pick your country, MoMo and banks, and a theme. Your accounts are created for you.

  2. Spend as usual

    Alerts are recorded as they arrive. Tip: put a category word in the payment reference.

  3. Review in Inbox

    Anything Pecunia couldn't sort waits in Inbox: choose a category once, or say “always”.

  4. See where it goes

    Charts, budgets and plans show where your money goes and what to change.

Your money data stays yours

Pecunia has no servers and no accounts of its own, and it shows no ads. Your transactions are saved either on your phone or, if you choose, in a spreadsheet in your own Google Drive. Nothing is sold or shared.

SMS and notifications. Pecunia only reads alerts that look like financial transactions, and only after you agree on its explanation screen and in Android's own permission prompt. Other messages and notifications are ignored and never stored.

Why Pecunia asks for Google access. Google sign-in is optional. If you choose Google storage, Pecunia asks only for access to files it creates (the drive.file permission), and uses it to keep your “Pecunia – Data” spreadsheet up to date and to open it on the website. It cannot see any other files in your Drive.
